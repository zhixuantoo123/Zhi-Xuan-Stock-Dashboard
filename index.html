CBOE Volatility Index (VIX) — Market Fear Gauge

VIX Numerical Regimes & Trading Interpretation
VIX < 15 : COMPLACENCY & CALM Expected Daily Move: < ±0.9%
Bull market regime. Hedging demand is low, option insurance is cheap. Ideal environment for trend-following equities and buying portfolio put hedges cheaply.
VIX 15 – 20 : NORMAL BASELINE Expected Daily Move: ±0.9% to ±1.25%
Historical long-term median. Balanced risk environment with normal market pullbacks and orderly corrections.
VIX 20 – 30 : ELEVATED ANXIETY & CHOP Expected Daily Move: ±1.25% to ±1.9%
Heightened institutional caution. Rapid intraday swings, wider bid-ask spreads, and options premiums rise. Tighten trailing stops and avoid excessive leverage.
VIX > 30 : EXTREME PANIC & CRISIS Expected Daily Move: > ±2.0%
Capitulation phase. High systemic stress, margin liquidations. Historically, extreme VIX spikes above 35-40 mark premier long-term generational buying opportunities for patient capital.
